# iCloseLeads Product Context

## Product

iCloseLeads is a client-acquisition workspace for freelancers and small agencies. It combines local business discovery, remote and live job leads, decision-maker research, proposal preparation, outreach tracking, and a lightweight CRM.

## Primary Users

- Freelancers looking for qualified client opportunities
- Small agencies running repeatable outbound workflows
- Operators who need to move from a lead signal to a researched contact and tracked follow-up

## Core Workflow

1. Find a relevant local business, remote role, or live opportunity.
2. Qualify the lead and identify the likely decision-maker path.
3. Prepare a contextual proposal or outreach message.
4. Save the lead, follow up, and track pipeline movement.

## Dashboard Requirements

- Dense enough for repeated daily work, but easy to scan.
- Clear distinction between page canvas, controls, cards, and results.
- Light and dark modes are separately designed scenes, not color inversions.
- Local Business Leads is the primary lead workflow.
- Existing routes, permissions, usage limits, and data behavior must remain intact during visual work.
- Accessibility, responsive layout, and legible contrast are non-negotiable.

## Visual Direction

The dashboard should feel authored, calm, and operational: strong black-and-white hierarchy, flat bordered surfaces, restrained rounding, compact pill controls, a focused purple action color, and a lime signal color used selectively for positive data. Avoid decorative gradients, oversized empty space, nested cards, and generic AI-dashboard styling.
