# Backlink Operating Notes

The goal is not "more links." The goal is relevant links that strengthen iCloseLeads as the lead generation platform for freelancers and small agencies.

## Best Linkable Assets

- Web design leads guide.
- Businesses without websites research.
- Cold outreach scripts for freelancers.
- Remote job lead workflow.
- Free lead calculator.
- AI proposal templates.

## Prospect Types

- Freelancer resource pages.
- Web design agency blogs.
- SEO consultant tool lists.
- Remote work communities.
- Startup/freelancer newsletters.
- Indie founder directories.
- Podcast/show-note pages where the founder can contribute practical outreach advice.

## Ahrefs Backlink Workflow

1. Export competitor backlinks from Ahrefs.
2. Filter out spam, coupon, unrelated SaaS, and AI-generated directories.
3. Keep only pages where iCloseLeads can add genuine value.
4. Record the angle:
   - replacement resource
   - expert quote
   - data contribution
   - template contribution
   - product listing
5. Do not send outreach until the target, pitch, and sender identity are reviewed.

## Outreach Quality Bar

Every pitch should explain why that specific page's readers benefit. No mass-blast templates.
