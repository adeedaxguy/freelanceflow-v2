## Search Intent

The searcher wants a reliable way to find businesses that may pay for website work. They do not want vague advice like "post on LinkedIn." They want specific sources, filters, scripts, and a repeatable workflow.

## Angle

Most web designers do not have a design problem. They have a timing problem. iCloseLeads helps them find businesses showing visible signals: no website, outdated website, public phone, local category fit, owner path, and a clear pitch angle.
